Default fees in merchant cash advance contracts

Most MCA contracts list fees that kick in if you miss payments, bounce a debit, switch bank accounts or otherwise break the agreement. These can add thousands of dollars to what the funder says you owe.

What to look for in your contract

  • Flat or percentage default fees added to your remaining balance.
  • Returned payment fees for each debit that bounces.
  • The full balance coming due at once after a default, instead of the regular daily or weekly payments.
  • Collection and attorney fees you may be asked to cover.

Why acting early matters

Once default fees are added and a lawsuit is filed, the amount the funder claims usually grows quickly. Reaching out before you miss payments, or right after, usually leaves more room to work out a lower payment.

Whether a default fee can actually be enforced is a legal question. Courts sometimes refuse to enforce fees that work as penalties rather than real estimates of the funder's loss.
