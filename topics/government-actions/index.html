Attorney general and FTC actions against MCA funders

State attorneys general and federal regulators have brought some of the biggest cases against MCA funders. These cases show what regulators consider illegal, and some have wiped out debts for thousands of businesses.

Major cases

  • New York Attorney General vs. Yellowstone Capital (2025): a $1.065 billion judgment and settlement. More than $534 million in debts owed by over 18,000 small businesses was canceled, and the companies were banned from the MCA business.
  • New York Attorney General vs. Richmond Capital Group: in 2026, a New York appeals court held the company's MCA agreements were properly characterized as loans subject to usury limits.
  • New Jersey Attorney General vs. Yellowstone Capital (2023): a $27.375 million settlement over abusive lending, servicing and collection practices.
  • FTC vs. Yellowstone Capital: more than $9.8 million paid over withdrawals taken without permission, with refund checks sent to 7,731 businesses.
  • FTC vs. RCG Advances: the company and its owner were permanently banned from the MCA industry.

What about the CFPB?

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's 2026 small business lending data rule excludes merchant cash advances, so most MCA oversight now comes from state laws, state attorneys general and the FTC.
