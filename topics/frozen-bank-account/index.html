MCA funder froze your bank account

A frozen account can stop payroll and bills overnight. With MCAs, it usually happens after the funder gets a judgment, and in New York the freeze can happen without a judge signing anything.

How the freeze works in New York

  • After a judgment, a restraining notice under CPLR 5222 can be issued by the court clerk or by the funder's attorney.
  • Your bank must hold up to twice the amount of the judgment.
  • Some funders also send UCC lien notices to your customers or card processor, telling them to pay the funder directly.

What to do

  • Get a copy of the notice your bank received and the case it's based on.
  • Check whether you were ever properly served with the lawsuit. If not, the judgment may be challenged.
  • Move fast. A freeze often pushes businesses into a bad settlement, and having an attorney involved changes the negotiation.
