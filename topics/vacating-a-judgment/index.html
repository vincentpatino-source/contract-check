Vacating a judgment from an MCA funder

Many business owners first learn about an MCA lawsuit when their bank account is frozen. If a judgment was entered against you without a real chance to respond, it may be possible to have it vacated.

If you never got the papers (New York CPLR 317)

If you were served some way other than having the summons handed to you or your designated agent, and you didn't respond, New York lets you ask to defend the case within one year after you learn of the judgment, and no more than five years after it was entered. You need to show you didn't actually receive notice in time and that you have a meritorious defense.

Other grounds

  • Excusable default: a court can also vacate a default for a reasonable excuse, usually within one year after you're served with notice of the judgment.
  • Confessions of judgment: these can sometimes be challenged, and some states don't allow them at all. Florida treats one signed before a lawsuit as void, and Texas voids MCA contracts that contain one.
  • Usury: if the MCA was really a criminally usurious loan, that can be part of the defense.

Time matters

Deadlines run from when you learn about the judgment, so the sooner you act, the more options you have.
