RICO claims against MCA funders

RICO is a federal law best known for organized crime cases, but it also covers collecting an "unlawful debt", including a debt that's usurious under state law. Some business owners have used it against MCA funders.

A key example

In Fleetwood Services v. Richmond Capital Group, a Texas business sued its MCA funder in federal court in New York. The court found the MCA was really a loan at a criminally usurious rate and ruled for the business, and in 2023 the federal appeals court for the Second Circuit affirmed.

What it takes

  • Showing the MCA was really a loan, not a sale.
  • Showing the rate was at least twice the legal limit under the governing usury law.
  • Tying the funder, and sometimes its owners, to collecting that debt.

RICO claims are complex and expensive, and they're not right for every case. But the possibility can change how a funder approaches a settlement.
