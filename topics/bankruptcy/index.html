Bankruptcy and merchant cash advances

Bankruptcy is a last resort, but it's worth understanding, because MCAs are treated very differently depending on whether a court sees them as loans or as sales.

The automatic stay

Filing for bankruptcy generally stops collection right away, including lawsuits, debits and freezes, while the case is pending.

Why the loan-or-sale question matters

  • If the MCA is a true sale: the funder may claim it owns the receivables it bought, which can complicate your use of incoming cash.
  • If it's really a loan: the funder is treated as a creditor, its claim can be restructured, and usury defenses may apply.

In 2026, a New York bankruptcy court recharacterized 19 merchant cash advance agreements as usurious loans.

Before you file

Many businesses can avoid bankruptcy by restructuring their MCA payments. It's worth a free review before taking that step.
