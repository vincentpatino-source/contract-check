Who we are

DebtMedx ("we") operates this website. You can reach us at 1-862-MCA-HERO (1-862-622-4376).

Contracts you upload

When you check a contract, your file is read in your browser and sent to our AI service provider to produce your report. DebtMedx does not receive your documents or report unless you choose to unlock your full report. When you unlock it, you give us your name and mobile number (and optionally your email) and agree to share your information with DebtMedx. A copy of your report and uploaded documents is then sent to DebtMedx and stored in our Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive), so a specialist can contact you about a free consultation. We keep it confidential and limit access to DebtMedx staff.

Information you give us

If you request a review, we collect what you enter in the form, such as your name, business name, phone, email, details about your advances and any files you choose to attach. We use it to contact you about your request and to provide our services. It is stored with our form provider (Tally) and in a spreadsheet our team uses.

Calls, texts and messages

When you unlock your full report, you agree that we may contact you about a free consultation by phone, text or email. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text, asking us on a call, or calling us at 1-862-MCA-HERO (1-862-622-4376).

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar tools, including Google advertising and analytics tags, to understand site traffic and show our ads to people who visited this site. You can limit this through your browser settings and the ad settings offered by Google.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run this site and our services, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you gave us by calling us at 1-862-MCA-HERO (1-862-622-4376). Residents of some states have additional rights under state law.
