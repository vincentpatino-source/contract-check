Virginia's rules for merchant cash advances

Virginia was one of the first states to regulate merchant cash advances directly, with a sales-based financing law in effect since July 2022.

  • Cost disclosures before you sign. Funders must give you a written disclosure on sales-based financing deals of $500,000 or less. Funders that make five or fewer deals with you in a year are exempt.
  • Funders and brokers must register. Providers and brokers of sales-based financing must register with the Virginia State Corporation Commission (SCC).
  • No APR required. Virginia doesn't require funders to show an APR.

Updated November 2026. Laws change, so confirm current rules with the state or an attorney.

Why an MCA can cost so much

A merchant cash advance is written as a purchase of your business's future sales, not a loan. Because it isn't a loan, it generally isn't subject to the interest-rate limits that apply to loans. That is how the cost can run so high even though the contract never calls it interest.
