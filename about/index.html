About DebtMedx

DebtMedx helps small business owners who are buried in merchant cash advances, reverse consolidations and high-cost business loans. We show you what your debt is really costing you, then connect you with the right people to fix it.

Why we exist

Merchant cash advances are sold as fast, easy funding. The contracts tell a different story: factor rates that work out to triple-digit costs, fees buried in the fine print, daily debits that drain cash flow, and pressure to take a second or third advance to cover the first. Many owners don't see the full picture until they're already behind.

We built DebtMedx so any business owner can see that picture clearly, for free, and then get real help from people who handle MCA debt every day.

What we do

  • Free contract check. See the true cost of every advance, including fees, total payback and estimated APR.
  • A full review of your advances. We look at all your contracts and recent bank statements together, including how much of your revenue is going to payments.
  • Lower payments with complete legal protection. Experienced MCA restructuring specialists work with your funders to lower your payments, typically 50 to 80% below what you're paying now, and complete legal protection is included.
  • An attorney licensed in your state. We assign you an attorney licensed in your state to work on your case.
  • Fees are built into the savings. On average, our clients pay less in total than what their funders claim they owe, and that includes everything: our fees, attorneys, processors, negotiators and everyone else involved in settling the debt.

Who we help

Small and mid-sized business owners anywhere in the United States who have one or more merchant cash advances, a reverse consolidation, or high-cost business loans. That includes owners who are current but struggling, owners who have fallen behind, and owners dealing with frozen accounts, customer notices or funder lawsuits. Loan or merchant cash advance, it doesn't matter: both can be restructured, and the goal is always to protect the business.

How it works

  1. Tell us what's going on. Which funders you have, what you're paying and how often, and any notices, deadlines or frozen accounts.
  2. We review your documents for free. Your contracts, payment history and any notices or court papers.
  3. We walk you through your options. A lower payment, typically 50 to 80% less than you pay now, with complete legal protection and an attorney licensed in your state assigned to your case.
  4. You decide. Nothing happens without your OK. Our fees are built into your savings and explained up front.

What we're not

We are not a lender, and we don't sell you another advance. We are not a law firm: DebtMedx is an educational referral service and does not provide legal, financial or tax advice. Legal work on your case is handled by an attorney licensed in your state.
