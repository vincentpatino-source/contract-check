    Questions

    Is this really free?

    Yes. Checking your contracts costs nothing. If you'd like help exploring options afterward, you can ask for a free review.

    Who sees my contracts?

    Your contract is read by our AI to build your report. DebtMedx only receives your report and documents if you choose to unlock your full report and share your information. We keep them confidential and never sell your information. See our privacy policy.

    How accurate is the APR?

    It's an estimate based on the dates and amounts in your contract, assuming payments continue as scheduled. Merchant cash advances often have no fixed term, so treat it as a guide.

    What's a reverse consolidation?

    An advance where the funder pays you in weekly installments, often to cover other advances, while you make payments back. We show both sides of the schedule. Read: Reverse consolidations explained →

    Can an MCA be illegal?

    Sometimes. If a court finds an MCA is really a loan in disguise, and its rate is above the legal limit, it may not be enforceable. Read: Is your MCA a loan in disguise? →

    Should I ask my funder for reconciliation?

    Not before you read the fine print. Some contracts treat a lower payment or a missed step during reconciliation as a default that adds fees. Read: The catch behind lower payments →

    I've been sued or my account is frozen. What now?

    Move fast. Lawsuits come with short deadlines, and a frozen account can sometimes be challenged. Read: Served with an MCA lawsuit → · Frozen bank accounts →

    Is this legal advice?

    No. It's an educational review. For legal questions about your agreement, talk to a licensed attorney.
