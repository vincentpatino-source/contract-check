About InstaFunders

InstaFunders (instafunders.com) is a business funding company based in Bay Harbor Islands, Florida, reported as founded in 2021. It provides funding of roughly $25,000 to $500,000 by purchasing a percentage of a business's future sales, with approval based on cash flow rather than credit history.

  • Based in: Bay Harbor Islands, FL
  • Founded: 2021
  • Offers: Merchant cash advances

Paying more than one funder?

Many business owners with a InstaFunders advance are also paying other funders. Funders often fund businesses that already have other advances, even when their own contracts ban stacking. Looking at all your advances together is the first step to a payment you can afford.
